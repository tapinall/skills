# Content gaps

The outcome is `tap-suites/social/<slug>/gaps.md`: a ranked list of gaps a producer can act on, each traceable to rows in `research.md` or entries in `tap-suites/social/learnings.md`.

A gap is demand the evidence shows, met by supply that is weak. Both halves need evidence. An idea with no demand evidence is not a gap; list it under "untested ideas" at most, because ranking it beside real gaps misleads the person who spends money on it.

## Kinds of gap

| Kind | The evidence looks like |
|---|---|
| Topic | Demand signals for a subject no competitor covers |
| Question | A question repeated in comments or Reddit that no post answers well |
| Format | Covered only in a format the audience does not consume on that platform, e.g. long articles where comments ask for quick tips |
| Angle | Everyone takes the same side or tone; the contrarian, insider or beginner angle is open |
| Depth | Only surface-level takes, with comments asking "how exactly?" |
| Freshness | The best coverage is outdated by a product, rule or trend change |
| Platform | A topic outperforms on one platform and is absent on another the audience also uses |
| Proven pattern | A hook or format outperforms across two or more accounts and the brand has not used it |
| Own proven pattern | A pattern in `learnings.md` that beat the owner's own median more than once and has not been pushed further |

Own proven patterns are the strongest evidence available, because they were measured on this audience, this account and this voice. A competitor pattern that contradicts one loses to it.

Also keep an avoid list: competitor themes posted often that underperform their own baseline, and patterns `learnings.md` marks as failed. They show where effort meets no response.

## Scoring

Score each gap 1–5 on three axes and rank by the product:

- Demand: how strong and repeated the evidence is, and whether it is rising.
- Supply weakness: how poorly existing posts serve it, from their outlier scores and the comments on them.
- Fit: whether the brand has the right to win here. Score it from the profile's proof bank: a gap the brand can fill with its own story, number, client or opinion scores high; one it could only fill with generic advice scores 1, because that piece would fail the only-us test in `references/anti-slop.md` and the platforms' originality rules.

Break ties by production cost, cheaper first. Every score cites the research rows, learnings entries or proof-bank items behind it; a score with nothing behind it is a guess and is marked as one.

Check the "Not collected" section of `research.md` before calling supply weak. A platform that was never fetched shows no supply because nothing was looked at.

## gaps.md

- A ranked table: rank, gap in one line, kind, demand, supply weakness, fit, score.
- Per gap: what the audience wants and the proof (links); why current supply fails (links); the proof-bank item or research finding that makes it only-us; the recommended platform, account mode and format; the angle; what the content is built to earn, whether shares, saves, comments or follows.
- The avoid list, each line with its evidence.
- Untested ideas, if any.

Show the ranked table and wait for the user to choose which gaps go to production.
