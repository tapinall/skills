# Personal brand or company brand

A founder's profile and a company page are rewarded for different things, so the same story is written, placed and measured differently on each. Settle the mode per platform before choosing benchmarks, formats or voice, and record it in `brief.md`.

## Choosing the mode

Start from the profile's Identity type:

- **Founder or freelancer**: the person's own profile is the primary channel. A company page, if any, amplifies: team, news, product, and reshares of the founder's best posts.
- **Company brand with a public face**: run both. The founder or an expert carries the stories and opinions; the page carries what only an organisation can say and features named people.
- **Company brand with no public face**: the page alone, built around named employees and customers rather than the logo.

The gap is large. In a study of 5,546 LinkedIn posts from YC W26 founders and their company pages, founder originals drew 6.9× the median reactions of company-page originals (62 vs 9) (PersonaBox, vendor, Feb 2026; correlational, many lifts confounded by YC mentions). Metricool's 673,658-post LinkedIn study found similar impressions per post but a 2.60% engagement rate for personal profiles against 1.60% for pages, 63% higher (vendor, 2026). The widely quoted "561% more reach" and "8× engagement" figures have no traceable primary source; do not cite them.

## Personal profile

- **Lead with first-person stories.** Narrative posts drew +96% median reactions over non-narrative posts once YC mentions were removed (PersonaBox). The proof bank's stories and defended opinions belong here.
- **Keep links and asks out of the post body.** External URLs and CTAs roughly halved engagement in the YC sample; Metricool found links cut personal-profile impressions 27% and interactions 20%. Put the link in the first comment or the profile, and let the CTA be the story's natural next step.
- **Write long, with a visual.** Posts over 1,200 characters drew +88% over posts under 100; images or carousels +144% over text-only, carousels +30% over single images (PersonaBox).
- **Comments are the currency.** Comments are about 20% of personal-profile engagement against 3% on company pages (LigoSocial, vendor), so personal posts can be built to start a substantive conversation the owner then joins.

## Company page

- **Feature a named person.** Posts naming a person drew +37% engagement, +63% on pages under 2,500 followers; team and culture posts +82% and company news +80% against the same page's product posts (LigoSocial, 5,530 posts from 200 pages, vendor, 2026).
- **Skip generic thought leadership.** It is the most common company-page post and the weakest, roughly a fifth below the page's average (LigoSocial; the figure is -22% or -24% depending on the page version). Opinion belongs on a person's profile, where it earns comments.
- **Build for shares and clicks, not comment threads.** Comments are a small share of page engagement; Metricool found pages earn more shares than personal profiles.
- **Links behave differently here.** Metricool found company-page posts with links gained 51% impressions and 41% interactions, the reverse of personal profiles; the YC sample still found CTAs halved page reactions (6 vs 13 median). Treat the link as fine and the hard ask as costly, then let the performance loop decide for this page.

## Voice

Personal: the owner's voice samples, "I", opinions they will defend in replies. Page: "we", with the named person quoted in their own words, and the same proof-bank entry told from the organisation's side. One story can run on both, the founder's version first and the page resharing or retelling it with the person named.

## Other platforms

No comparable personal-versus-company study was found for TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. On those platforms the same principles are hypotheses: a person on camera, first-person stories, one named human per company post. Run them, then let the performance loop measure them against the account's own median. Every platform demotes templated, unoriginal output (Meta 2026, YouTube July 2025, TikTok September 2026), which hits company accounts that recycle one format hardest; `references/anti-slop.md` covers the rules.

## Measuring

Score each account only against its own median, and compare competitors only within the same mode. A page at half a founder's engagement may be doing well for a page.
